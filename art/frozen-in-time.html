Frozen in time

Mixed media · 50cm (W)x 40 cm (H) · 2025

$400 AUD

Buy this painting

Feel the rush of winter with "Frozen in Time." This highly dynamic painting uses bold, textured strokes to bring a sweeping blizzard and ice-covered shoreline to life. Contrasted against the rich, deep blues of a freezing river, it perfectly captures the raw power and striking beauty of a landscape locked in winter's grip.
