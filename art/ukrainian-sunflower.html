Ukrainian Sunflower

Acrylic on canvas · 91cm (W) x 122 cm (H) · 2025

$810 AUD

Buy this painting

The inspiration of this piece was -Glory to Ukraine, a pieceful and beautiful country which was envaded by Russia. Ukrainians go through a lot of hardship, but don't surrender !!! I am amazed by their courage. They're defending their villages, cities, and an indepedent future for their children. The sunflower is the Symbol of Ukraine. In the center of the sunflower I created a miniature landscape of traditional Ukrainian village with small houses, a winding river, green hills, and tall cypress-like trees, to give the veiwers the idea about my Motherland.
