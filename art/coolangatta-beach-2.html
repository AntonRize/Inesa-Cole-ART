Coolangatta Beach 2

Acrylic on canvas · 139cm(W) x 99cm (H) · 2025

$780 AUD

Buy this painting

A dramatic seascape capturing the raw power of nature as storm clouds gather over turbulent waters. Bold brushstrokes convey the energy of the approaching tempest. I live in Coolangatta , close to the beach, and every time I go there I can see a a different picture: the sky, the sea change all the time, which is exciting and unpredictible. To capture that exact moment is blessing!
